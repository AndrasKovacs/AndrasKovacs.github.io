I've just started a postdoc with Jon Sterling, who encourages writing [weekly
notes](https://www.jonmsterling.com/01JR/) in the research group. I think that this is a good
thing. I've had periods in my life when I was doing regular public writing, and also had one streak
of [weekly talks](https://www.youtube.com/@andraskovacs9191/videos) in the Covid era. I enjoyed
these routines and I believe that they improved my productivity, but I've never stuck to them for
long. Now that I'm being incentivized by Jon, expect some blogging output here.
